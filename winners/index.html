Take 13 Events

31 Oct 2026

Halloween

Spooky season on campus

On 31 October, FLAME University is transported onto the set of a horror movie. Organised with the university’s Cultural Committee, the spooky decor took over the Plaza, Shantiniketan and Kalidas, and the ‘All Hallows Eve’ fair brought food, tarot and a lot of games. Every Thursday in October 2026, the newly launched ‘Thursday Talkies’ series screened a classic horror film for the film enthusiasts of FLAME.

25 Oct 2026

“So You Want To Make a Film?”

FirstCut x FLAME University Immersion Day

A Film Immersion Day curated by FirstCut with FLAME University’s Outreach Department. For the first time in 13 years, FirstCut opened its doors to 15 high-school students for an immersive session on the nuts and bolts of filmmaking: screenwriting, storyboarding and cinematography. The students took charge of a real film set and closed the day by creating a 6.5 minute short film together.

2 Dec 2026

Winter Carnival

FirstCut’s flagship event

Considered the home of FLAME’s holiday spirit, the Winter Carnival returns to deck the halls of FLAME University with funky club collaborations, engaging game stalls and interactive photo opportunities, plus live music performances, interactive design spaces and a midnight special screening. The warm, fuzzy feeling we call ‘Once Upon a Winter’.

9–11 Feb 2027

FirstCut Finale

The main days of Take 13

Our main days promise to be the most enthralling of them all, under our Take 13 theme, Celebrating Student Cinema. Each day holds screenings of shortlisted films across Nano, Animation, Fiction and Non-Fiction, workshops on direction, cinematography, sound design and more, and we will also host commercial film screenings and on-campus collaborations every day, aligned with our three sub-themes.

About FirstCut 2027 Take 13 +

Welcome to FirstCut, one of India’s largest international student film festivals, organized by FLAME University, Pune.

FirstCut provides a dynamic platform for young filmmakers across the globe, encouraging them to explore film as a medium of growth and change. In 2026, FirstCut Take 12 received 600+ submissions from students representing 70+ countries.

At the final event, shortlisted films will be screened for a live audience and evaluated by a distinguished jury from the Indian film industry. The screenings will be followed by engaging panel discussions and talks with renowned film personalities.

We believe creative freedom is essential, which is why participants have complete freedom in choosing their themes and styles. In addition to categories in fiction, animation, and non-fiction, FirstCut offers the unique "Nano" film category, challenging students to create super short films.

Awards, Prizes & Entry Fees +

Award: Winners in each category will receive a certificate and a cash prize of INR 30,000. Transaction charges will apply to international winners.

Please Note:

  • Submissions to FirstCut are FREE of charge.
  • Attending the event is FREE and open to all.
Eligibility, Student IDs & Category Time Limits +
  • Multiple Submissions: Students can submit multiple films across various categories.
  • Student ID: Submission of VALID STUDENT IDs IS MANDATORY at the time of film submission.
  • Category Time Limits:
    • Fiction: 20 minutes
    • Non-Fiction: 40 minutes
    • Animation: 10 minutes
    • Nano: 2 minutes
  • Eligibility for Crew: Directors, cinematographers, editors, scriptwriters, and sound designers must be current undergraduate or master’s students or recent graduates (graduated within the past year — 2026). Actors and other crew members do not need to be students. There’s no limit on the number of students from the same institution.
Production Guidelines, Subtitles & Completion Dates +
  • Films must be original, including all content like music, visuals, and scripts. Copyrighted material is not accepted.
  • Projection-quality picture and sound are preferred.
  • All films, including those in English, MUST have English subtitles.
  • The film’s content must adhere to legal standards (no obscenity, defamation, copyright infringement, or incitement of communal disharmony). The jury will make final decisions on content.
  • Completion Dates: Fiction, Non-Fiction, and Nano films must be completed on or after January 30, 2026. Animation films must be completed on or after January 30, 2025.
Submission Platforms & Accepted Link Formats +
  • Submit via FirstCut’s website or the FilmFreeway page. Submit on 1 platform only.
  • Films must be submitted as downloadable links via Google Drive, Vimeo, or Hidden YouTube.

Privacy Notice

How the FirstCut website uses your information. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Who we are

FirstCut is the international student film festival organized by students of FLAME University, Pune. Contact: firstcut@flame.edu.in.

What we collect and why

We collect the details you type into our forms so we can review your film, contact you about the festival, answer collaboration and sponsorship enquiries, and verify student status for submissions.

  • Film submission form: name, university, email, mobile number, city and country, film details, names of the director, cinematographer, editor and scriptwriter, their student ID cards, and the film link or file.
  • Outreach form: institution, city, contact person, email and area of interest.
  • Sponsorship form: brand or company name, contact person, corporate email, tier of interest and, optionally, a budget range.
  • Saved draft: your film form answers are kept in your own browser so they are not lost if the page closes. They stay on your device until you submit or clear them.

Uploaded student ID cards are used only to confirm student status for the submission.

Cookies and measurement

We use Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, and Cloudflare statistics to count visits and see which pages are used.

If you choose Accept on our cookie notice, we also use the Meta Pixel. It tells Meta that a visit or a form submission happened on our site, so we can measure our advertising. It does not receive what you type into our forms. If you choose Decline, the Meta Pixel does not load. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings.

Other services

Pages load fonts from Google, scripts from unpkg and cdnjs, and the aftermovie from YouTube. These providers may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you submitted by writing to firstcut@flame.edu.in.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.

What Each Department Does

Events

Logistics

Decor

The Pop of Colour we all need, Decor brings FLAME University alive with the spirit of FirstCut, turning academic spaces into portals of film.
