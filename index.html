Submission Categories

Fiction

Max 20 Mins

Fiction films often explore themes of human emotion, relationships, or imagination, using elements like dialogue, mise-en-scène, and dramatic structure. At FIRSTCUT, fiction shorts can be experimental or traditional, ranging from realistic portrayals of life to fantastical narratives, but must not exceed 20 minutes.

Non Fiction

Max 40 Mins

Non-fiction films include documentaries, biographical portraits, investigative works, or essay films. At FIRSTCUT, the non-fiction category provides filmmakers up to 40 minutes to capture truth-based stories whether shedding light on social issues, exploring cultural contexts, or offering personal perspectives grounded in reality.

Animation

Max 10 Mins

Animation films use frame-by-frame techniques to create the illusion of motion, employing styles such as hand-drawn, stop-motion, 2D digital, or 3D computer graphics. At FIRSTCUT, animated films must stay within 10 minutes, demonstrating how unique visual language can captivate, entertain, or even educate.

Nano

Max 2 Mins

The Nano category challenges filmmakers to tell compelling stories within an ultra-short format of just 2 minutes. These films can belong to any genre (fiction, non-fiction, or animation) but must rely on concise storytelling, striking visuals, and precise editing to create impact. Nano films highlight brevity as an art form, proving that a story doesn’t need to be long to be powerful.

Submissions FAQ

Can I submit a film that has already been screened at another festival? +
Yes, we accept films that have been screened elsewhere, provided the filmmaker holds all intellectual property rights and the film was completed within the eligible eligibility window (completed after January 1, 2024).
Do my films require subtitles? +
Yes! All films with dialogue in languages other than English must have burnt-in English subtitles or an attached `.srt` subtitle file. Subtitle compliance is automatically scanned during vetting.
Are there entry fees for student submissions? +
Submissions via Google Forms and direct college outreach channels are free of charge. Submissions through third-party platforms (FilmFreeway, FilmFest Platform) may incur nominal platform processing fees.
Is my submission format checked automatically? +
Yes. Our backend runs automated format and length validation. Submissions exceeding runtimes (Fiction: 20m, Non-Fiction: 40m, Animation: 10m, Nano: 2m) will trigger automatic compliance flags, and filmmakers will receive an automated follow-up notification.
