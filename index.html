About FirstCut 2027 Take 13 +

Welcome to FirstCut, one of India’s premier international student film festivals, organized by FLAME University, Pune.

FirstCut provides a dynamic platform for young filmmakers across the globe, encouraging them to explore film as a medium of growth and change. In 2026, FirstCut Take 12 received 700+ submissions from students representing 75+ countries.

At the final event, shortlisted films will be screened for a live audience and evaluated by a distinguished jury from the Indian film industry. The screenings will be followed by engaging panel discussions and talks with renowned film personalities.

We believe creative freedom is essential, which is why participants have complete freedom in choosing their themes and styles. In addition to categories in fiction, animation, and non-fiction, FirstCut offers the unique "Nano" film category, challenging students to create super short films.

Awards, Prizes & Entry Fees +

Award: Winners in each category will receive a certificate and a cash prize of INR 30,000. Transaction charges will apply to international winners.

Please Note:

  • Submissions to FirstCut are FREE of charge.
  • Attending the event is FREE and open to all.
Eligibility, Student IDs & Category Time Limits +
  • Multiple Submissions: Students can submit multiple films across various categories.
  • Student ID: Submission of VALID STUDENT IDs IS MANDATORY at the time of film submission.
  • Category Time Limits:
    • Fiction: 20 minutes
    • Non-Fiction: 40 minutes
    • Animation: 10 minutes
    • Nano: 2 minutes
  • Eligibility for Crew: Directors, cinematographers, editors, scriptwriters, and sound designers must be current undergraduate or master’s students or recent graduates (graduated within the past year — 2026). Actors and other crew members do not need to be students. There’s no limit on the number of students from the same institution.
Production Guidelines, Subtitles & Completion Dates +
  • Films must be original, including all content like music, visuals, and scripts. Copyrighted material is not accepted.
  • Projection-quality picture and sound are preferred.
  • All films, including those in English, MUST have English subtitles.
  • The film’s content must adhere to legal standards (no obscenity, defamation, copyright infringement, or incitement of communal disharmony). The jury will make final decisions on content.
  • Completion Dates: Fiction, Non-Fiction, and Nano films must be completed on or after January 30, 2026. Animation films must be completed on or after January 30, 2025.
Submission Platforms & Accepted Link Formats +
  • Submit via FirstCut’s website or the FilmFreeway page. Submit on 1 platform only.
  • Films must be submitted as downloadable links via Google Drive, Vimeo, or Hidden YouTube.
